### Features

* A named `click` or `jsclick`, including `text=`, also matches a tab, a radio, and a label. A label with `role=button`, `role=link`, `role=tab`, or `role=radio` is that control. A label with no role is used only when its control is a radio or checkbox, such as an antd Segmented item, and no button, link, tab, or radio has the name, so a field label does not take a same-name button. Nested candidates with the same name keep the innermost one, and a label that wraps or points at a disabled control is disabled. A word that is a CSS selector, such as `button`, stays a selector ([#747](https://github.com/EndeavorYen/chrome-cdp-ex/issues/747)).
