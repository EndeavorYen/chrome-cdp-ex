### Breaking Changes

* A cross-site `click`, `fill`, `jsclick`, or `select` receipt, and its disabled-control error, keeps the tag, the `@fN:M` ref, and the frame host (`Clicked <BUTTON> (@f2:1) in pay.example.com.`). The accessible name, label, option text, and cover text read from that frame are dropped outside a `FRAME CONTENT` block. `--since-action` wraps the cross-site part of the diff in the same block. `Kind` and `Next` stay as they were for those actions ([#638](https://github.com/EndeavorYen/chrome-cdp-ex/issues/638), [#724](https://github.com/EndeavorYen/chrome-cdp-ex/issues/724), [#727](https://github.com/EndeavorYen/chrome-cdp-ex/issues/727)).
