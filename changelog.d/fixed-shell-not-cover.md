### Bug Fixes

* `overlay` and a covered click no longer treat a full-viewport fixed app shell that contains the target as the cover, so Next is not `dismiss-modal` against that shell. The cover is the element over the hit point that does not contain the target, including an absolutely positioned popover. A real opaque full-screen cover is unchanged. A fixed ancestor behind the layer is that shell only when the layer is the sole rendered child on every step from the ancestor down to the layer. Siblings that are `display: none`, `visibility: hidden`, zero-size, or `script`, `style`, `template`, `head`, `link`, `meta`, or `noscript` do not count. A role-less full-viewport backdrop stays a blocking overlay when another rendered sibling is on the page, such as a corner panel or an iframe, even if only `html` and `body` are behind the centre, and `dismiss-modal` still reports `overlay-not-dialog` ([#629](https://github.com/EndeavorYen/chrome-cdp-ex/issues/629)).
